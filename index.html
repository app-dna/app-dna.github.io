What drives us

Three rules for every app.

Our ideas start with everyday moments that make you rack your brain. These three rules turn them into apps.

  1. 01

    Simplicity

    Built around the people who use it. Every app follows the same guidance-by-steps concept, so it is understood at first glance and quick to use.

  2. 02

    Applicability

    Each app starts from a real, frustrating everyday situation and focuses on solving that one problem properly.

  3. 03

    Efficiency

    No detours, no clutter. People who use our apps keep pointing to the guided flow as what they like most.
